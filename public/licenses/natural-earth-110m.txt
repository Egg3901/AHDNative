Natural Earth 1:110m admin-0 countries (public domain)
=====================================================

Asset: src/game/geo/countries-110m.json (TopoJSON Topology, 177 country
features, quantized arcs + transform).

Source: AHDGame public/geo/countries-110m.json at immutable rev
08820d108bf986d519aed28c2963690dd772c652, itself derived from Natural
Earth 1:110m admin-0 boundaries (world-atlas TopoJSON family). Copied
verbatim; no geometry edited, added, or repositioned.

Rights: Natural Earth data is public domain
(https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). No attribution
is legally required; this file records provenance anyway. No boreholes,
no third-party licensed shapes, no invented territories: dissolved
historical states with no single modern feature (Yugoslavia,
Czechoslovakia, East Germany) have no polygon in this asset and render
as an honest geometry gap, exactly as the AHDGame world globe treats
them (region-shard overlays, not base features).

Reference behavior preserved (AHDGame src/app/world/components/
WorldMapSVG.tsx + MapSVGContent.tsx at the pinned rev):
- /map redirects to the country-scoped /country/[code]/map.
- Only registered/mapped countries are clickable; unregistered land is
  drawn but inert.
- The offline port uses a flat equirectangular projection instead of
  the server globe's orthographic rotation (phone-first adaptation;
  layout may differ, selection behavior is preserved).

ISO numeric -> game CountryId mapping is ported from AHDGame
src/lib/worldCountryRegistry.ts (WORLD_COUNTRY_ISO_TO_ID) at the same
pinned rev; see src/game/worldGeo.ts.
